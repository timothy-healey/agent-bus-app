C · Kanban pipeline

Each team is a column. Task cards flow left-to-right through the pipeline. Gates appear as distinct columns with their queue. Clicking a card opens detail (log + artifact viewer). Worker count per team = card lane density. The pipeline shape is visible at a glance.

My recommendation: C. It's the only one that visualises the pipeline as the pipeline. Status of work-in-flight reads at a glance — much easier than tmux. Gates are first-class columns, so they stop being a hidden side-channel. Click to drill into any card for logs + the artifact it produced.
